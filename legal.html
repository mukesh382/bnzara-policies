Bnzara Legal Policies
↩️

Return Policy

▼
  • Items can be returned within 7 days of delivery
  • Product must be unused and in original packaging with all tags intact
  • Damaged or defective items are eligible for immediate return or replacement
  • Refund will be processed within 5–7 business days after return is received
  • To initiate a return, contact our support team with your Order ID
  • Items marked as non-returnable on the product page are not eligible for return
🚚

Shipping Policy

▼
  • Standard delivery: 3–7 business days
  • Express delivery: 1–2 business days (available in select cities)
  • Free shipping on orders above ₹499
  • Shipping charge of ₹49 applies on orders below ₹499
  • You will receive a tracking link via SMS/email once your order is shipped
  • Delivery to remote areas may take additional 2–3 days
❌

Cancellation Policy

▼
  • Orders can be cancelled before they are shipped
  • Once an order is shipped, cancellation is not possible
  • To cancel, go to My Orders and tap Cancel Order
  • Refund for cancelled orders will be processed within 3–5 business days
  • Prepaid orders will be refunded to the original payment method
  • Cash on Delivery orders have no refund applicable on cancellation
