Return Policy

▼

Return Window

  • Items can be returned within 24 hours of delivery ONLY
  • Return request must be initiated within 24 hours via the app
  • After 24 hours, returns are NOT accepted
  • Return window is strict and non-negotiable

Return Eligibility

  • Product must be unused and in original packaging
  • All tags and seals must be intact
  • No signs of wear, damage, or use
  • Damaged or defective items are eligible for immediate return or replacement at no cost

Non-Returnable Items

  • Grocery & Food Items: NO returns accepted (fruits, vegetables, dairy, packaged items, spices, snacks, beverages)
  • Perishable or consumable products (food, cosmetics)
  • Items that have been tampered with or damaged due to customer misuse

Return Process

  • Go to My Orders → Select order → Tap Return Item (within 24 hours)
  • Provide reason for return
  • Return request must be initiated within 24 hours of delivery
  • Once approved, pick-up will be scheduled within 24 hours
  • Item must be returned in original, unused condition with all packaging intact

Refund Timeline

  • Return request must be initiated within 24 hours of delivery
  • Once approved, item must be returned within 24 hours
  • Refund processed within 3–5 business days after return is received and verified
  • Amount credited to original payment method (Wallet or Card/Bank)
  • Wallet refunds appear instantly

Delivery Policy

▼

Delivery Coverage & Time

Bnzara delivers only within 1–2 km radius from our store.
  • Average delivery time: 15 minutes
  • Maximum delivery time: 30 minutes
  • Delivery times may vary based on traffic, distance, and item availability
  • All deliveries are within store's service radius only

Delivery Charges & Platform Fees

  • Delivery Charge: Store-specific (shown at checkout)
  • Platform Fee: Store-specific (shown at checkout)
  • Free delivery offers may apply based on store promotions
  • All charges are clearly displayed before order confirmation

Order Tracking

  • Real-time order status available in the My Orders section
  • Track order from "Placed" → "Confirmed" → "Out for Delivery" → "Delivered"
  • No separate SMS/Email tracking (check app for live status)

Delivery Address

  • Delivery address must be within 1–2 km of the store
  • Ensure accurate address with complete details (building, flat no., landmarks)
  • Delivery boy will contact you if address is unclear

Failed Delivery Attempts

  • If delivery boy cannot reach you, we will attempt redelivery
  • If delivery cannot be completed within 6 hours, order will be automatically cancelled
  • Refund will be processed to your original payment method
  • Contact support for manual intervention if needed

Cancellation Policy

▼

How to Cancel

  • Open the app → My Orders
  • Select the order you want to cancel
  • Tap on Help tab → Select Cancel Order
  • Provide reason (optional)
  • Confirm cancellation

When Can You Cancel?

  • Orders can be cancelled before delivery boy is assigned
  • Once delivery boy is on the way, cancellation may not be possible
  • Contact support immediately for urgent cancellation requests

Refund After Cancellation

  • Wallet Payments: Instant refund to wallet
  • Card/Bank Payments: Refund within 3–5 business days to original payment method
  • Cash on Delivery: No refund applicable (order not taken)
  • Processing time may vary based on your bank

Automatic Cancellation

  • Orders automatically cancelled if delivery boy is not available within 6 hours
  • You will receive a cancellation notification in the app
  • Refund will be automatically processed
