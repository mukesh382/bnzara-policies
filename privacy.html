Privacy Policy

1. Information We Collect

When you use TurantBuy, we collect only what is needed to take and deliver your order:

  • Your name and mobile number
  • Delivery addresses you save, including the location you pick on the map
  • Your current location, only while you are choosing a delivery address or store
  • Order history
  • A device identifier, used to keep your account signed in on one device at a time

We do not ask for your email address, and we do not collect photos, contacts or any media from your phone.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To place, prepare and deliver your orders
  • To show you the correct store for your area
  • To let the delivery person reach your address
  • To answer your questions and handle complaints
  • To keep your account secure and prevent misuse

We do not use your data to show advertisements, and we do not build advertising profiles.

3. Third-Party Services

We do NOT sell, trade or rent your personal information to anyone.

The app runs on the following services:

🔥 Google Firebase
Used for: Mobile number verification (OTP login) only
Data: Your mobile number
Privacy: Firebase Privacy Policy
🛡️ Cloudflare
Used for: Our database, product storage and app servers
Data: Account details, addresses, orders, product catalogue
Privacy: Cloudflare Privacy Policy

These providers process data on our behalf under their own data protection agreements.

4. Information Sharing

We share only the minimum needed, and only with:

  • The store fulfilling your order — your order items and delivery details
  • The delivery person — your name, address and mobile number
  • Payment gateway — only if you pay online; we never receive your card details

5. Data Security

  • All data travels over encrypted HTTPS connections
  • Our servers and database are protected by Cloudflare
  • Login is by one-time password sent to your mobile number — no password to leak
  • Only authorised staff can access customer data, and only for support work

6. Location Data

We ask for location permission so you can pick your delivery address on the map and so we can show the store that serves your area.

  • Location is used only while the app is open — never in the background
  • You can refuse or turn off location permission at any time from your phone settings
  • If you turn it off, you can still enter your address manually

7. Device Permissions

TurantBuy asks for only one permission:

  • Location — to pick your delivery address and find your nearest store. Optional; the app works without it.

The app does not request camera, photos, contacts, microphone or storage access.

8. Your Rights

You can, at any time:

  • See and correct the details saved in your profile
  • Delete your account and the data linked to it
  • Ask us for a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Withdraw your consent for us to process your data

To delete your account, open the app and go to Help → Delete Account, or write to us at help.bnzara@gmail.com.

9. Data Retention

  • Your profile and saved addresses are deleted when you delete your account
  • Order and billing records are kept as long as tax and accounting law requires
  • We do not keep data longer than needed

10. Children's Privacy

TurantBuy is meant for users aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If we learn that we have, we will delete it.

11. Payment

  • Online payments are handled entirely by the payment gateway
  • We never see or store your card number, UPI PIN or bank credentials
  • Cash on Delivery involves no online payment data at all

12. Where Your Data Is Stored

Our servers are operated by Cloudflare and mobile number verification is handled by Google Firebase. Both are global services, so your data may be processed on servers outside India under their standard data protection safeguards. By using TurantBuy you consent to this.

13. Third-Party Links

The app may link to outside websites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices — please read their policies before sharing anything.

14. Policy Updates

We may update this policy when our practices or the law change. Important changes will be shown in the app. Continuing to use TurantBuy means you accept the updated policy.
