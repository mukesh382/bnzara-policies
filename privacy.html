Privacy Policy

1. Information We Collect

When you use Bnzara, we collect information you provide directly to us, including:

  • Full name and phone number
  • Email address
  • Delivery addresses and location data
  • Payment information (processed securely via third-party processors)
  • Order history and preferences
  • Device information (device ID, OS version, app version)
  • App usage data and analytics
  • Product images and photos (if uploaded)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Process and deliver your orders
  • Send order confirmations and delivery updates
  • Respond to customer inquiries and support requests
  • Improve our app, services, and user experience
  • Send promotional offers and discounts (you can opt out anytime)
  • Analyze app usage and performance trends
  • Prevent fraud and ensure account security
  • Detect nearby stores based on your location

3. Third-Party Services & Data Processors

We use trusted third-party services to deliver our app safely and securely.

Your data is processed and stored with the following services:

🔥 Google Firebase
Used for: User authentication, real-time database (Firestore), cloud storage, analytics
Data: User accounts, order data, app preferences, analytics
Privacy: Firebase Privacy Policy
☁️ Cloudinary
Used for: Image and media hosting, optimization, and delivery
Data: Product images, user photos
Privacy: Cloudinary Privacy Policy
🛡️ Cloudflare
Used for: DDoS protection, content delivery network (CDN), security
Data: Website traffic, IP addresses
Privacy: Cloudflare Privacy Policy

Important: We do NOT sell your data to any third party. These services are bound by data processing agreements to protect your information.

4. Information Sharing

We do NOT sell, trade, or rent your personal information.

We only share limited information with trusted partners:

  • Delivery partners (delivery address and contact only)
  • Payment processors (payment data only, never stored by us)
  • Cloud service providers - Firebase, Cloudinary, Cloudflare (for secure infrastructure)

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information:

  • 256-bit TLS/SSL encryption for all data in transit
  • Encryption at rest in Firebase secure servers
  • DDoS protection via Cloudflare
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • Restricted access to personal data (admin only)
  • Secure Firebase authentication with multi-factor support
  • Firestore security rules (database-level protection)

6. Location Data

We collect your location data to:

  • Detect your nearest store
  • Provide accurate delivery estimates
  • Improve delivery route optimization

You can disable location access in your device settings at any time. This may limit certain features of the app.

7. Cookies and Tracking

Bnzara uses cookies and analytics to:

  • Remember your preferences and login status
  • Track app performance and usage patterns
  • Provide personalized recommendations
  • Improve user experience

Firebase Analytics collects anonymized usage data to help us improve the app.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal information
  • Update or correct your information
  • Delete your account and associated data
  • Opt out of promotional communications
  • Request a copy of your data
  • Withdraw consent for data processing

To exercise these rights, contact us at support@bnzara.com.

9. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to provide our services. After account deletion:

  • Account data is permanently deleted from Firebase
  • Order history may be retained for legal/tax compliance (anonymized)
  • Analytics data is aggregated and anonymized

10. Children's Privacy

Bnzara is not intended for users under 13 years old. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that a child has provided personal information, we will delete it immediately.

11. Device Permissions

Bnzara requests the following device permissions:

  • Location: To detect nearby stores (can be disabled)
  • Camera: For delivery code scanning (optional)
  • Photos: To upload receipts/images (optional)
  • Contacts: To auto-fill addresses (optional)

12. Payment & Billing

Payment processing is handled by third-party payment gateways. We:

  • Do NOT store complete credit card details
  • Process payments securely via PCI DSS compliant processors
  • Use tokenization for recurring payments
  • Never see your full card number

13. Third-Party Links

Bnzara may contain links to external websites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices. Always review third-party privacy policies before sharing information.

14. International Data Transfer

Your data is stored on Google Cloud Platform (Firebase) which may involve transfers across countries. By using Bnzara, you consent to such transfers under appropriate data protection safeguards.
