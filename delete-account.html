How to delete your TurantBuy account

You can ask us to delete your account and the data linked to it at any time. There are two ways.

From inside the app

  1. Open the TurantBuy app
  2. Go to Profile → Help
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm

Without the app

Send us an email or a WhatsApp message containing:

  • Your registered mobile number
  • The words "Delete my TurantBuy account"
📧 support@turantbuy.com
📱 WhatsApp: +91 88904 84141

What is deleted

Once the request is confirmed, we permanently delete:

  • Your name and registered mobile number
  • All saved delivery addresses
  • Your account and login record
  • Your cart and saved preferences

What is kept

Past order and billing records are retained for up to 8 years, as required by Indian tax and accounting law. Your name and mobile number are stripped from these records, so they can no longer be linked back to you.

How long it takes

Deletion requests are completed within 7 working days. We may call your registered number once to confirm that the request is genuine.

Deletion cannot be undone. After your account is removed, your order history is no longer visible to you, and you will need to register again to place a new order.
